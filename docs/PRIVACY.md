# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

> © 2026 Brainstorming Studios. All rights reserved.

Brainstorming Games is run by Brainstorming Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the site, why, and the choices you have. We built the site to work with as little personal data as possible.

## The short version

- You can play without an account. Guests are identified only by a random id stored in your browser.
- We ask for a birth year, not a birth date, and only when you create an account.
- If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must approve your account before social features switch on.
- Children and anyone whose age we do not know never get personalised ads.
- You can download or delete your account data at any time from Settings.

## What we collect

### If you play as a guest

- **A random anonymous id** kept in your browser's local storage. It is not linked to your name, e-mail or device identifiers.
- **Play events** tied to that id: which game was opened, how long it was played, whether an ad was shown, the type of device (desktop, mobile or tablet) and your country (derived from your IP address at the edge; the IP address itself is not stored with events).
- **Your favourites, recently played list and votes.** Favourites and history stay in your browser. A like or dislike is stored with your anonymous id so it can be counted once.

### If you create an account

- **E-mail address**, or the identifier from Google or Discord if you sign in with them.
- **Username and display name** that you choose.
- **Birth year**, used only to decide whether parental consent is needed and which ads you may see.
- **Progress data**: Brain Points, quests, streak, unlocked cosmetics, leaderboard scores, cloud saves and your friends list.

### If you are a developer

- Studio name, country and a contact e-mail.
- Payout details (bank or payment-service information). These are encrypted before storage and can be read only by authorised finance staff.

### What we do not collect

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, address, precise location, contacts or photos. We do not use fingerprinting. We do not sell personal data.

## Why we use it

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Run the site and load games | Anonymous id, device type | Legitimate interest |
| Measure which games people enjoy | Play events | Legitimate interest |
| Share ad revenue fairly with developers | Ad events per game and country | Contract with developers |
| Accounts, friends, parties, leaderboards | Account and progress data | Contract with you |
| Show ads | See "Advertising" below | Consent where required |
| Keep the service safe | Reports, rate-limit counters | Legitimate interest |

## Children and teenagers

Brainstorming Games is designed to be safe for players aged 12 and over.

- When you sign up we ask for your birth year. If you are, or may be, under 18, your account starts in a restricted state.
- We send a consent link to the parent or guardian e-mail address you give us. Until they approve, you can play, but friends, public profiles, party chat and named leaderboard entries stay off.
- We do not track the behaviour of minors for advertising and we never show them personalised ads.
- A parent or guardian can withdraw consent or ask us to delete a child's account at any time by writing to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

This flow follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules.

## Advertising

- Ads appear around games and, in some games, between levels.
- **Non-personalised by default.** Guests, minors and anyone using school-safe mode only ever see non-personalised (contextual) ads.
- **Personalised ads** are shown only to signed-in adults, and in the EU, EEA and UK only after you agree in the cookie banner.
- Our ad partner (Google) may use cookies. You can change your choice at any time under Cookie preferences.

## Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage for your preferences (theme, language, low-data mode), your anonymous id, and your guest favourites and history. Advertising cookies are set by our ad partner and follow your consent choice. See Cookie preferences for details.

## Who we share data with

- **Infrastructure providers** that host the site and database on our behalf (Cloudflare, Supabase).
- **Our advertising partner** (Google), for showing and measuring ads.
- **Developers** see only aggregated statistics for their own games and the bug reports you send them. They never receive your e-mail address.
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

- Play events: 13 months, then they are deleted or reduced to daily totals.
- Account data: until you delete your account.
- Payout records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
- Parental consent records: for as long as the child's account exists, plus the period the law requires.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

- **Access and export** your data: Settings, then "Download my data".
- **Delete** your account and its data: Settings, then "Delete account".
- **Correct** your username or display name in Settings.
- **Withdraw consent** for personalised ads under Cookie preferences.
- **Complain** to us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}, and to your local data-protection authority. In India you may also approach the Data Protection Board.

## Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Games run in a sandboxed frame on a separate domain and cannot read your account. Access to the database is restricted row by row, so a player can only read and change their own data.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will announce it on the site before the change takes effect.
