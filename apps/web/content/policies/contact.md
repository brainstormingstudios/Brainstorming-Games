# Contact

We read every message. To help us answer quickly, pick the route that fits.

## A problem with a game

Open the game and use **Report a bug** under the game frame. Your report goes straight to the developer together with the technical details they need.

## Players and parents

Questions about accounts, privacy, parental consent or anything else: write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Parents and guardians can use the same address to withdraw consent or ask us to delete a child's account. Please write from the e-mail address that approved the account so we can confirm it is you.

## Developers

Questions about publishing, the SDK or payouts: write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with your studio name in the subject. The [Developers page](/developers/) answers the most common questions.

## Legal and copyright

If you believe a game on the site copies your work, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the title of the game, a link to your original work and your contact details. We pause the game while we check.
