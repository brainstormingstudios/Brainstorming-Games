# Info for parents

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Brainstorming Games is a free website where children and adults play browser games. This page explains, in plain language, how we keep it suitable for younger players.

## What your child can see

- Every game is reviewed by a person before it goes live and must be suitable for ages 12 and over.
- Games cannot contain gambling, real-money prizes, entry fees or paid "loot box" rewards.
- Games run inside a protected frame. They cannot see your child's account, e-mail or browsing.

## Accounts and your consent

- No account is needed to play.
- If your child creates an account, we ask only for a birth year.
- For anyone under 18, the account starts restricted. We e-mail the parent or guardian address your child gives us with a link to approve or decline.
- Until you approve, your child can play games but cannot add friends, have a public profile, use party chat or appear by name on leaderboards.
- You can withdraw consent or ask us to delete the account at any time. See the [Contact page](/contact/).

## Talking to other players

- There is no free-text chat. Party rooms use a fixed list of friendly phrases and emoji.
- Friends are added by username or invite link, and either side can remove the other.

## Ads

- The site is free because it shows ads.
- Children, and anyone whose age we do not know, are only ever shown non-personalised ads. We do not build advertising profiles of minors.
- Ads never cover a game while it is being played.

## Money

- Nothing on the site can be bought by a player today.
- Brain Points are earned by playing and only unlock avatar decorations. They cannot be bought, sold or cashed out.

## Tools you can use

- **School-safe mode** shows only games flagged as school-safe, turns social features off and needs a PIN to switch off. See [School-safe mode](/school-safe/).
- **Low-data mode** keeps downloads small on mobile connections.

## Questions

Write to us through the [Contact page](/contact/). Our [Privacy policy](/privacy/) has the full detail.
