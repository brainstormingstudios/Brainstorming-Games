# Cookie preferences

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

This page explains what Brainstorming Games stores in your browser and how to change your choices.

## What we store ourselves

We use your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies, for things the site needs to work:

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Preferences | Theme, language, low-data mode, school-safe mode | Until you clear it |
| Anonymous id | Counts plays and votes once, without knowing who you are | Until you clear it |
| Favourites and history | Your saved and recent games as a guest | Until you clear it |
| Sign-in session | Keeps you logged in, if you have an account | Until you log out |
| Referral code | Credits the developer whose link brought you here | 30 days |

None of these follow you to other websites.

## Advertising cookies

Ads are provided by Google. Depending on your choice and your age, Google may set cookies to show and measure ads.

- **Guests, under-18s and school-safe mode:** non-personalised ads only. These still use cookies for basics such as limiting how often you see the same ad.
- **Signed-in adults:** personalised ads only if you have agreed. In the EU, EEA and UK we ask first and the default is "no".

## Changing your choice

You can change your advertising choice at any time from the cookie banner link in the footer. Clearing your browser's site data resets everything on this page.
