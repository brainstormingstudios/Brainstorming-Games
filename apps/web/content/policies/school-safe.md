# School-safe mode

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

School-safe mode turns Brainstorming Games into a calmer, filtered version for classrooms, libraries and shared family devices.

## What changes when it is on

- **A filtered catalogue.** Only games our reviewers have flagged as school-safe are shown: no combat-focused games, nothing frightening.
- **No social features.** Friends, parties and public profiles are hidden.
- **Contextual ads only.** Ads are never personalised in school-safe mode.
- **A PIN to turn it off.** Whoever switches it on chooses a PIN. Without it, the mode stays on.

## How to turn it on

School-safe mode is set per browser. Open Settings, switch on School-safe mode and choose a PIN. It then applies to everyone who uses that browser, signed in or not.

## For schools and IT teams

- The site works without accounts, so pupils do not need to hand over any personal data.
- All pages are served over HTTPS from a single domain, and games from one separate play domain, which makes allow-listing simple.
- Low-data mode can be switched on alongside school-safe mode to reduce bandwidth.

## How games are chosen

A game is flagged school-safe by a human reviewer during quality review, not by the developer. If you see a game that does not belong, use **Report a bug** on the game page and choose "Inappropriate content".
